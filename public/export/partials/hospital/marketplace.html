How External Consultations Work

1. Request

Search and request a specialist. Patient stays at your hospital as the care zone.

2. Consult

Doctor joins via video or visits in person. All records go to patient's EMR at your hospital.

3. Revenue

Default split: 30% to your hospital, 70% to the specialist. Your hospital still generates income.
